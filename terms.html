Memories are personal

Kakera helps you gather what you and your family remember. Memories, especially early ones, can be incomplete or mistaken, and different people can remember the same day differently. Kakera keeps every version side by side and doesn't decide which is right.

Looking back can stir up strong feelings. Kakera is not therapy and does not replace a doctor, counsellor or therapist. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number.

Other people's stories

When you add what someone told you, or a photo of someone, please treat it with care and share it only in ways they would be happy with.
