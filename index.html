Kakera privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Who we are

Kakera is made and published by ATMZ FOREVER LLC ("we", "us"). Kakera helps you collect the memories of your early years, and what your family remembers about them, on a timeline by age. You don't need an account. Kakera has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send anything to us or anyone else, and it has no analytics, tracking or ads.

What is stored on your phone

Kakera keeps everything you enter in the app's private storage on your phone: your memories and the versions other people told you, the ages you guess, places, feelings and short names, the names of the people you add, your voice recordings, copies of the photos you pick, and your settings. We never receive any of it.

Voice and speech

When you record, Kakera asks Android for the microphone and saves the recording on your phone. To write your words down, Kakera hands the sound to your phone's own offline speech recognition (on most phones, Google's speech service running on the phone). Kakera only uses offline speech recognition, so the sound is not sent over the internet for this. If your phone can't do it offline, Kakera keeps the recording without the words.

Photos

You choose photos with Android's photo picker, so Kakera only sees the photos you pick, never the rest of your gallery. Kakera saves a smaller copy of each; your originals are not changed.

Backups

If Android's own backup is on for your phone, it can include your memories' words and your people, encrypted in your Google account, under Google's terms. Voice recordings and photos are left out of that backup because of its size limit. Moving to a new phone with Android's transfer tool brings everything across.

Kakera's "Back up to a file" makes one file with your memories, people, recordings and photos, and saves it only where you choose (for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive). That file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. "Restore from a backup" reads such a file back.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a memory, Kakera makes a picture of it and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. When you make a PDF, it is saved only where you choose. Kakera sends nothing by itself.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. Kakera only learns whether the check passed; it never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN. While the lock is on, Kakera also asks Android to hide its screens from screenshots and the recent-apps view.

Permissions

Microphone (asked the first time you record): to record your voice memories.

Fingerprint / face (never asked; Android lets apps use it): only for the app lock, if you turn it on.

Kakera has no internet, location, contacts or full photo access.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share your information. We don't receive your memories, recordings, photos or anything else you put in Kakera.

Children

Kakera is meant for people 13 and older and is not directed at children.

Deleting your information

Delete a memory or a version from its edit screen; its recordings and photos are deleted with it. Clearing Kakera's storage in Android's settings (Settings > Apps > Kakera > Storage > Clear storage), or uninstalling the app, deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you made are yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update and is posted online with a new effective date.
